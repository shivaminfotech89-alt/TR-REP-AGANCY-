## 1.0 Period of contract

Two years from execution of agreement (07.09.2026). Extension on mutual
discussion and as per agreement, based on performance, field office comments and
suggestions. Clause 16.0 repeats this and adds that UGVCL may cancel in between.

**App:** contract window is 07.09.2026 to 07.09.2028. Clause 39.0 governs the
boundary — see below.

## 2.0 Rates

As per Schedule-A attached. Accepted percentage: **7.00 % above the estimated
rate of UGVCL** for CRGO / Amorphous core. Rates exclusive of GST, net of
salvage value.

**App:** store base rate and the 7 % separately. See `ugvcl-2026-schedule.txt`.

## 3.0 Transformer rating covered

- 11 KV / 250 V, 1-phase — 5 KVA
- 11 KV / 433 V, 3-phase (stack/wound/dry/SDT/PAT/any other) — 10 to 500 KVA
- 22 KV / 433 V, 3-phase (stack/wound/dry/SDT/PAT/any other) — 16 to 500 KVA
- 11/22 KV, 3-phase amorphous — 5 to 500 KVA
- Special design transformers

Sealed or bolted type, copper or aluminium winding, from any distribution circle
as decided by UGVCL. Actual quantity and rating allotted varies with performance,
repair speed, technology adopted, testing instruments and economical quality of
repair.

## 4.0 Estimate approving authority

Full extract in `ugvcl-2026-estimate-approval.txt`. Summary:

- Inspection witnessed by UGVCL representative; **transformer must not be opened
  in their absence**; work starts only after estimate approval.
- Joint internal inspection by EE (O&M) + one EE/DE of Circle office + JE (TMS).
  **Videography mandatory.**

## 5.0 Factory set-up

- Shared equipment/instruments between two or more repairing agencies is not
  permitted; observed → allotment stopped and contract cancelled.
- Separate entity required: separate shed (not same premises), separate
  ownership, different partners/proprietor, separate 20 KW electricity
  connection, different qualified engineers, separate equipment. Anything found
  common → contract cancelled.
- JE / DE Division office must report agencies sharing equipment to corporate.
- Minimum 3000 sq ft built-up area with open land for new bidders. Leased
  premises need at least 5 years remaining on the registered lease deed from
  tender date, with separate road entry. Existing agencies with less area that
  do not supply on time get annual allotment reduced proportionately.

## 6.0 Technical know-how

At least one full-time qualified engineer with minimum one year experience must
supervise. Every test certificate signed by the agency's qualified engineer.
Engineer name, qualifications, photo and experience to be in the offer. Engineer
absent during factory visit → contract cancelled.

## 7.0 Shifting of premises

Same circle or other circle: Rs. 1500 registration charge, joint factory
inspection by EE-rank corporate and circle engineers, approval by the concerned
circle office before any transformers are issued at the new premises. Shifting to
another circle additionally requires completing all OGP transformers issued by
the original circle first; GP-failed transformers of the old circle carry
transport charges borne by the repairer, deducted from bills. GST applies to
registration charges.

## 8.0 Change of partners

Sale of agency or addition/deletion of partners requires fresh partnership deed
and power of attorney to the circle office, copy to CE. Change of name,
ownership or control → process fee Rs. 5000 recovered.

## 9.0 Instruments

Any instrument replaced, changed or sent out for repair during the contract must
be intimated immediately to the CE, circle and divisional head, or the contract
is liable to cancellation for full or part period. Factory shed with sufficient
open land, minimum 20 KW LTMD or NRGP connection, all machinery, testing
equipment and a permanent qualified engineer required throughout. Failure →
cancellation and GP-failed transformers repaired at the agency's risk and cost
through another agency until guarantee period ends. NABL calibration insisted
every year and on repair of an instrument.

## 10.0 / 36.0 Transportation

**Inside UGVCL jurisdiction:** UGVCL arranges transport both ways and handles
loading of failed and unloading of repaired transformers at field. The agency
bears unloading of failed and loading of repaired transformers at its own works,
at its cost.

**Outside UGVCL jurisdiction:** the agency arranges transport both ways and
bears to-and-fro charges; UGVCL still handles loading/unloading at field.

**Guarantee-period failures:** lump sum **Rs. 275 per transformer** recovered
against to-and-fro transportation.

**App:** Rs. 275 is a recovery line against the agency, triggered by GP failure.

## 14.0 Performance guarantee

Paid to the concerned SE, UGVCL circle office, by DD from a scheduled commercial
bank favouring UGVCL payable at the circle head quarter, or by bank guarantee
from a nationalised bank per the prevailing Gujarat Govt circular, prior to order
placement.

- Total = 10 % of tender value, recovered from all successful agencies after
  equal splitting among existing bidders. As the tender is for two years it is
  half of 10 %, i.e. 5 % of tender value = **Rs. 2,30,000** (subject to minimum
  Rs. 1.5 lac if applicable).
- Validity **43 months** for CRGO / amorphous / CRGO wound core (24 months work
  order + 18 months guarantee + 1 month claim), **31 months** for SDT/PAT
  (24 + 6 + 1).
- Outage period — from date of receipt of the transformer until it is repaired —
  is **not counted** toward guarantee; guarantee extends by the outage period and
  the PG must be extended to match.
- Returned one month after expiry of the guarantee period of the last repaired
  transformer. DD retained till completion of guarantee period. No interest.
- Failure to pay security deposit in time → EMD forfeited and contract cancelled.

**App:** outage period extends guarantee. That makes date-of-receipt and
date-of-repair-complete load-bearing fields, not just informational.

## 15.0 Insurance

Rs. 10 lac per repairer, submitted to the concerned SE within one month of

Contractor maintains all pertinent and legally obligatory insurance throughout;

## 24.0 Damaged parts

Damaged HV and LV coils are **retained by the repairer after cutting into four
pieces** so they cannot be reused, in the presence of UGVCL's representative,
with details recorded in the joint inspection report signed by both.
**Videography of cutting the damaged winding is mandatory** during internal
inspection.

## 25.0 Winding and coil identification

(a) New coils replacing damaged ones must use similar quality material and
insulation. Insulation of wire/strip, inter-layer between LV and HV, and windings
to earth must be **class 'A'**, section as used by the original manufacturer. If
improper size or type of material is found, the contract is cancelled immediately
and ongoing payments withheld.

(b) The repairer must insert a **non-tearable polyester strip** bearing the
company name and month of manufacture at **at least 2 places in each HV/LV coil**,
not removable on pulling. This lets UGVCL verify the exact number of coils newly
prepared and replaced.

**App:** the tender's own mechanism for counting replaced coils. Relevant to how
a coil count on the estimate can be substantiated.

## 26.0 Change in number of coils

Coil design must follow the original design. The repairer may not change it
without written approval of the competent authority.

## 27.0 Transformer oil

- Failed transformers are supplied either full to the oil level gauge mark or
  totally empty. Whether full or empty must be recorded in the physical and
  internal inspection report.
- Maximum **5 % losses** allowed towards filtration / impregnation / wastage for
  oil filled in failed transformers.
- **No filtration or impregnation loss** allowed for transformers failed in
  guarantee period, or where fresh oil is given.
- Used carbonised oil taken out of the failed transformer is filtered free of
  cost and used by the agency. Filtered oil is tested electrically, must meet
  required BDV, and is filled after repair. Oil is tanked after proper cleaning
  of the tank and removal of sludge.
- Fresh oil filling requires the **presence of JE / DE**; quantity clearly
  mentioned in the report and signed by the concerned JE/DE and the agency's
  authorised representative.
- Scrap oil with sludge disposed of by the repairer at own risk.

**App:** ties to the existing per-division oil carry-forward. The 5 % cap and the
zero-loss GP case are two different rules on the same field.

## 28.0 Work allotment

- Initial quantity decided by CE UGVCL on established repair capacity, upgraded
  technology, past performance and timely return of GP-failed transformers.
- **Subsequent allotment only after 90 % of the previously allotted lot is
  returned repaired**, plus performance and failure percentage. Below 90 % → no
  further allotment until the 90 % is received.
- Failure rate above **15 % (11 KV)** or **20 % (22 KV)** → further allotment at
  UGVCL's discretion or stopped.
- JE/DE (TMS) reviews agency performance in monthly TMS MIS reports to circle
  office; circle reports to corporate, signed, by the **10th of every month**.
- Separate allotment for 11 KV and 22 KV based on past performance and failure
  percentage. Field issues strictly by class — an 11 KV agency must not be issued
  22 KV transformers and vice versa.
- UGVCL may allot any number of any capacity against poor performance, or stop
  dealing with a poorly performing agency or one in breach of trust.

**App:** the 90 % return gate and the 15/20 % failure ceilings are computable
from job data and are the kind of thing that should be visible before the
division raises it.

## 29.0 Drying of active parts

Oven drying to the specified limits to remove all moisture. Records to be kept
for every active part: estimate, weight of transformer, date placed in oven, date
removed, megger value. **Oven records not found during checking → contract
cancelled.**

## 30.0 Painting

Tank internally painted with anti-corrosive hot-oil-resistant special paint that
does not affect transformer oil. Externally one coat zinc chromate anti-corrosive
paint plus two coats Admiral Gray. Tank surface cleaned properly before zinc
chromate. UGVCL symbol, 4 inch, painted red on both sides.

## 31.0 Testing after repairs

Routine tests per IS 1180 and IS 2026:2014 before delivery, arranged by the
repairer. **Seven days' advance written intimation** to the company's
representative before repaired transformers are ready for inspection and testing;
failing that, the repairer is responsible for delay and penalty under clause 37.
Testing in presence of company representatives; **results recorded in a register**
and a test witness report signed jointly. Components must conform to relevant BIS
and be of standard/reputed make; certificates and purchase evidence produced on
demand. Agency must have facility for all acceptance tests, with all instruments
calibrated annually at a government approved laboratory.

Tests per IS 1180 / IS 2026 on each transformer:

a. Winding resistance
b. Voltage ratio and check of voltage vector relationship
c. Impedance voltage / short circuit impedance (principal tapping where
   applicable); no-load losses and current
d. Insulation resistance
f. Induced over voltage withstand (double power and double frequency)
g. Separate source voltage withstand (HV test) — HT 28 KV for 11 KV, 50 KV for
   22 KV, LT 3 KV for one minute
h. BDV test for oil

*(lettering as printed — there is no item "e")*

### Loss tolerances

(a) For **5, 10, 16, 25, 63 and 100 KVA** of 11/22 KV: the figures are maximum
losses, so **no tolerance is permissible** per IS 2026 **for first-time
repairing**. For **second and subsequent repairs**, a further maximum **10 % (+)**
on losses is allowed.

(b) For **200 and 500 KVA** of 11/22 KV per IS 2026:
  (i) **+15 %** on each of no-load and full-load losses, provided tolerance on
      **total** losses does not exceed **+10 %**.
  (ii) Tolerance on % impedance as per IS 2026.

Every repaired transformer is routine tested. The agency maintains a register per
transformer; measured no-load and load losses must be entered. The register is
checked by the inspecting officer at any time and produced for verification.
Register without proper details → UGVCL's action is binding on the agency.

For **DTR older than 20 years**, some deviation is allowed for all capacities per
IS 1180 / IS 2026. Guaranteed losses for transformers with loss capitalisation
formula are as declared by the manufacturer, available on the nameplate or from
the respective A/T.

**App:** tolerance depends on repair count and rating band. This is the second
place repair count changes behaviour.

A UGVCL team may visit the works at any time to verify estimates/assessment,

## 37.0 Delivery, interest and penalty

Timeline:

| Step | Limit |
|---|---|
| Joint inspection from date of sending transformers | 10 days |
| Repairer sends estimate from date of joint inspection | **2 days** |
| Division JE (TMS/Store) prepares release after receipt of estimate | 3 days |
| Circle office conveys approval of estimate/release | 4 days |
| Corporate office conveys approval | 15 days |

If the estimate is not received within two days of internal inspection, the
Division Office issues notice to the agency. JE TMS follows up with
circle/corporate for approval after preparing the release. The concerned JE
arranges required oil within a short period from estimate approval.

(A) Repair within **30 days** from date of approval of estimate for OGP, and
within 30 days from date of receipt of transformer for GP-failed. No transformer
kept unrepaired beyond that, failing which the repairer may become liable to
**interest at 15 % per annum on capital locked** — computed on the purchase price
of the Board/Company for each rating, taken from the nearest date of A/T for new
transformers — from the date of completion of the granted period, for transformers
with no oil pending to issue to the agency.

(B) Same 30 days, failing which a **15-day notice** is issued by the Division
office, and if still not repaired within 15 days, penalty at **½ % per week plus
applicable taxes on the repairing cost** of the transformer under repair is
recovered without further notice, for transformers with no oil pending to issue.
For GP, the repairing cost may be the last repaired cost.

**Clarification:** the ½ % per week penalty is recovered after **45 days**
(30 days + 15 days notice) from date of approval of estimate for OGP, and from
date of receipt for GP, for transformers with no oil pending to issue. Effective
delivery period is therefore **45 days**.

**App:** the "no oil pending to issue to agency" qualifier is a precondition on
the penalty clock. A job waiting on oil from the division should not accrue
penalty. That interacts directly with the existing oil carry-forward logic.

## 38.0 Guarantee

38.1 Guaranteed technical particulars and performance must be maintained for
repaired transformers except where voltage ratio is to be changed. The value of
percentage impedance, losses and efficiency shall not be altered from original
except when voltage is changed. Items used must be of standard specification.

38.2 Guarantee from date of dispatch, for the whole unit irrespective of parts
repaired or replaced:

| Type | Guarantee |
|---|---|
| 11 KV transformers, 5 to 500 KVA, CRGO core | 18 months |
| 11 / 22 KV, 5 to 500 KVA, amorphous core | 18 months |
| 22 KV transformers, CRGO core | 12 months |
| 11 / 22 KV SDT / PAT, various ampere ratings | 6 months |

At joint inspection the repairer must check each coil and each part in the
presence of UGVCL's engineers while submitting the estimate. **No extra charges
are allowed** for a transformer failed within guarantee even if damage is found
beyond the parts repaired or replaced. GP failures must be repaired within
**30 days** of intimation by the concerned EE; otherwise further joint inspection
of other deposited transformers will not be carried out until the GP repairs are
done. **Only external physical inspection** is carried out for GP failures — no
internal inspection.

**App:** guarantee runs from **date of dispatch**, extended by outage period per
clause 14. A GP job must price to zero and must block joint inspection of other
units once it passes 30 days.

## 45.0 GP failure scrapped due to core damage

If during guarantee the core is found damaged, or the transformer is otherwise
unrepairable and must be scrapped, the **total amount of the last repairing bill
is recovered from the repairing agency**, and no other charges are paid — no
opening, no labour.

Since no internal inspection happens in the presence of UGVCL for GP failures, if
the agency finds core damage on initial opening it must **not disturb anything**
in the transformer — HV/LV coils, core etc — and must immediately call the
concerned EE of the division office for internal inspection.

If a GP repaired transformer fails **within 6 months** of installation, the
contract clause applies. If it fails **after six months, no recovery applies** and
the transformer is declared scrap.

If UGVCL's inspection finds the core or HV/LV coils **disturbed by the agency**,
**50 % of the cost of the transformer purchased in the last A/T is recovered**.
Joint inspection report to be prepared in detail. All such cases with joint
inspection report may be referred to CE UGVCL, whose decision binds the agency.

The company has every right to carry out internal inspection of the job and
**stage inspection during repairing**.

**App:** three distinct recovery amounts — full last bill, nothing, or 50 % of a
new transformer — selected by whether the failure was inside 6 months and whether
the agency disturbed the internals.

## Open items this A/T settles or affects

- **sealType (as-received vs as-delivered):** clause 32.0 says two seals are
  provided by Divisional JE TMS after repair and testing, opened by UGVCL at
  internal inspection on failure, then fresh seals provided by UGVCL after the
  next repair. Sealing is UGVCL's act at both ends. Clause 3.0 separately calls
  the transformer "sealed or bolted type" as a construction attribute, and
  Schedule-A item 17 pays 1524 for converting sealed to bolted — so "sealed" is
  both a construction type that can change during repair and a physical seal
  applied by UGVCL. These are two different things and probably need two fields.
- **O21, missing originals:** Schedule-A items 12B and 13B are the published
  rates for when original coils are missing, at materially higher per-kg rates
  than 12A/13A. Clause 20.0 covers the related case of a missing nameplate.
- **O29, revised figure approval:** clause 4.0 routes approval by percentage
  band, and clause 11.0 makes payment follow the bill submitted on completion.
  The A/T does not directly state what happens when UGVCL approves a figure
  different from the submitted estimate. Still open.
- **Rollover:** clause 39.0 is the rate-boundary rule — old rate applies where
  the transformer was delivered before expiry of the order and joint inspection
  is complete.
