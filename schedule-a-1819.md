# Schedule-A — A/T 1819

Rate schedule attached to A/T No. UGVCL/EE-T-1/TRANS-REP/2026-28/01/AT/1819
dated 07.09.2026 (ZENITH TRANSFORMERS). Companion to `1819AT.md`.

**SCHEDULE-A (UGVCL-2026) — item-wise rate for repairing of damaged 11/22 KV,
5 to 500 KVA CRGO (stack/wound/dry/PAT/SDT) / amorphous core dist. transformers.**

## Before using any rate

- All rates below are **base UGVCL rates**. The accepted percentage on this A/T
  is **7.00 % above** the estimated rate of UGVCL. Store base and percentage
  separately — the percentage is A/T-specific.
- Rates are **exclusive of GST**. GST escalation during the contract is paid by
  UGVCL as statutory variation on production of document.
- Rates are **net of salvage value**.
- A rate of **0 means not payable in that band** — treat as refuse, not as free.

## KVA bands

Every banded rate has six columns in this order:

| Band | Covers |
|---|---|
| B1 | 5 KVA |
| B2 | 10 & 16 KVA |
| B3 | 25 KVA |
| B4 | 50, 63, 75 KVA |
| B5 | 100 KVA |
| B6 | above 100 KVA |

A rating falling in no band (30 KVA, 150 KVA) must refuse, not round.

## Formulas

**Coil line (items 12 / 13)**

```
coil_amount = weight_kg × material_rate        (12A/12B/13A/13B)
            + weight_kg × winding_labour_rate  (12C/13C)
```

Material rate is chosen by originals-missing + material + S.E. Labour rate is
chosen by material only.

**Re-insulation line (item 14)**

```
reinsulation_amount = weight_kg × 102 or 116
```

Item 14 is an alternative to replacement on that limb, not an addition. A limb is
either re-insulated (item 14, no winding labour) or replaced (12/13 plus 12C/13C
labour). Never both on the same limb. See `1819AT.md` for the caveat — the A/T
does not state in words whether item 14 is labour-inclusive.

**Percentage and GST**

```
billed_total = Σ(line amounts) × 1.07
then GST added as a separately claimed item on the bill
```

**Clause 4.0 threshold amount**

```
threshold_amount = Σ(line amounts EXCLUDING items 18a, 18b and 20) × 1.07
```

Strip the tank, conservator and radiator lines **before** applying the
percentage. Do not subtract the bare rates from an already-grossed-up total —
that leaves the percentage share of those lines behind. Compare
`threshold_amount` against the 25 % table in clause 4.0 to determine the
approving authority.

## Rate lookup rules

1. Band by rating only. Unbanded rating → refuse.
2. A 0 is a refusal. Breather at 5 KVA and tap changing switch at 5/10/16/25 KVA
   must be blocked from being added at all, not added at zero.
3. Item 20 in B6 needs the exact rating.
4. Coil rates need the S.E. flag. If it is not set, block the estimate — do not
   default to "without S.E.", which under-bills.
5. The 7.00 % applies on top and is stored separately from the rates.
6. Estimates must be able to present material cost, GST and labour separately.
7. Print the Sr. No. (12A-b1, 13B-a and so on) against each coil line so a
   division office can see which of the eight rate rows was applied.

## Conflict with the photographed fixed-rate pages

Separate photographed pages headed "AMORPHOUS // CRGO Wound CORE TRANSFORMER"
price winding as a **fixed rate per transformer** by rating and material, with
some rows per coil plus 2345 labour, and give tank/conservator at 54/kg and
radiator at 1057 / 1256 / 1452.

This Schedule-A prices coils **per kg** and gives tank/conservator at 55/kg and
radiator at 1061 / 1061 / 1258 / 1458.

They do not agree and they number their items differently — the photographed
page refers to "Item No. 3 to 6 of this schedule" for tank/conservator/radiator,
while clause 19.0 of this A/T points at "Sr. No. 18a, 18b & 20 of Schedule-A".
Clause 19.0 matches this file.

Do not merge the two, do not average them, and do not implement both pricing
paths as live. This file is the schedule attached to A/T 1819.
