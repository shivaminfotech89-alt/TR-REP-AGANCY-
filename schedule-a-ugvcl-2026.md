## Item 7 — Overhauling (separate rate sheet)

"Overhauling of complete transformer" is billed **as per Sr. No. 21 of
Schedule-A** above, per each transformer. Scope covers opening/closing/refitting
including minor repairing works, loading & unloading, repairing of tanks &
radiators by welding to stop oil leakage, replacement of burnt/damaged external
parts (bushing, nut-bolts, breather), dismantling of bushing, replacement of all
old gaskets by new, opening welding of top cover plate, un-tanking of winding,
removal and reassembly of core plate assembly, replacement of all types of
insulation, replacement of explosion vent diaphragm, testing, cleaning of tank,
removal of sludge, filtration of oil, strengthening/brazing of winding joints,
fitting/tightening of internal joints at HV/LV bushing, internal painting,
drying of active parts to proper IR value, and fixing of name plates.
Same 7.00 % above estimated rate applies.
